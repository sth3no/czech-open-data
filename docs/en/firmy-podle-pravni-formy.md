# Number of companies in the Czech Republic by legal form

How many limited liability companies (s.r.o.), joint-stock companies (a.s.), associations, cooperatives and other legal entities exist in Czechia: counts from the Czech Statistical Office's Register of Economic Subjects by legal form, total and active.

The Register of Economic Subjects holds 882,590 active legal entities (natural persons excluded), of which 583,021 are limited liability companies (s.r.o.) and 27,386 joint-stock companies (a.s.).

- **Data as of:** 31 October 2026
- **Rows:** 67

Natural persons (sole traders) are excluded. Active = the entity has no dissolution date in the register; it does not prove actual business activity.

## Columns

| Column | Description |
| --- | --- |
| `pravni_forma_kod` | Legal form code (Czech Statistical Office), e.g. 112 = s.r.o. (limited liability company), 121 = a.s. (joint-stock company) |
| `pravni_forma` | Legal form name (Czech) |
| `subjektu` | Number of entities in the register including dissolved ones |
| `aktivnich` | Number of active entities (no dissolution date) |
