# Currently unavailable medicines in the Czech Republic by ATC group

How many medicinal products currently have interrupted or discontinued supply to the Czech market, by anatomical ATC group.

On the data date, 1,524 medicinal products (SÚKL codes) have interrupted supply and 12,948 have discontinued supply.

- **Data as of:** 9 November 2026
- **Rows:** 14

Based on marketing authorisation holders' reports to SÚKL. An interruption of one pack variant does not mean the medicine is unavailable: another pack size or a substitute is often available.
