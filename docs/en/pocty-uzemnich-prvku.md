# How many municipalities, cadastral areas, streets and addresses are in the Czech Republic

Counts of territorial units of the Czech Republic from the RÚIAN register: regions, districts, municipalities, municipality parts, cadastral areas, streets, postcodes and address points.

Czechia has 6,258 municipalities, 13,074 cadastral areas, 15,106 municipality parts, 85,997 streets and 3,022,397 address points.
